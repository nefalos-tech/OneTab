## Evidence plan

[Desktop acceptance](../evidence/desktop-acceptance.md) holds the button and flow checklist, the local audit results, the presentation fixes, the limitations, and recommended decisions for F6, B5, B6, P6, and the live wallet PR. Its hosted rows stay **NOT RUN** until their handoffs exist. Each row names the handoffs it needs from this list:

- B5's public deployment, with `APP_ORIGIN` set to the URL the demo records.
- A deployed revision that includes Payments PR #38, which merged into main on 2026-11-08.
- The live wallet PR, the F2 and F4 remainders in this lane, now in progress on `feat/F2-live-wallet`.
- P6's three verified payer profiles and the separate seller.

Each Done when item has its place in that record. The checklist and the limitations are in its flow checklist and limitations sections. The legible receipt and failure are in its capture and failure sections, at the window size its browser table records. The fresh page load is in its reload section. Until the hosted evidence exists, F6's pull request says `Refs #12`, not `Closes #12`.

Before the hosted run, F6 fixed three findings from the local audit. Two were should-fix: **Approve in wallet** sat below the fold at 1280 by 800 (A2), and the creation-unavailable screen had no next step (B1). One was a nit: the focus ring faded in from white (A7). F6 also reworded two design-system rules to match the code (A8 and B4). The payment and room contracts are unchanged.
