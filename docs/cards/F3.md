Estimated effort: 3 hours. This is a planning budget, not a delivery guarantee.
Phase: Build

## Outcome

An organizer creates a fixed checkout and friends join it through a shareable link.

## Start and handoffs

Build forms and room rendering using RoomSnapshot fixtures; wire real routes when available.

Implementation prerequisites: [F1](https://github.com/nefalos-tech/OneTab/issues/7)

Needed for final integration: [F2](https://github.com/nefalos-tech/OneTab/issues/8), [B3](https://github.com/nefalos-tech/OneTab/issues/15).

## Work

- [ ] Collect title, seller wallet, positive USDC total and two or three payers. Show a review step and exact shares before creating.
- [ ] Keep seller, total and participant count immutable once created. Explain that corrections require a new checkout.
- [ ] Treat the organizer as the first payer. Let distinct wallets with the link claim remaining slots.
- [ ] Show seller, total, all participant wallets, individual shares and the viewer's own contribution.
- [ ] Handle invalid input, duplicate wallet, full room, missing checkout, loading and request errors. Copy the share link.

## Done when

- [ ] An organizer and two friends can enter the same room using its link.
- [ ] The organizer counts toward the payer limit and no wallet claims two spots.
- [ ] Refresh preserves the room and its immutable payment details.
